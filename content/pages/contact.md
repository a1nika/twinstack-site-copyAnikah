## What to include

A short line on the problem you're solving, roughly where you are in the
process (idea, existing org, existing package), and any deadline that matters
— Security Review submission date, a renewal, a launch.

## What happens next

Replace this with the real answer: who reads the message, how fast they
reply, and whether the next step is a call or a written scope.
